- Normative contract: `docs/adr/0050-cocodex-fail-safe-proxy-injection.md`.
- `src/codex/injection-guard.ts` fails closed unless:
  - the listener is on the configured port;
  - `/healthz` identifies the proxy and reports a PID;
  - that PID passes expected-process verification; and
  - a viable, enabled, running persistent service or healthy enabled shim exists.
- `src/codex/inject.ts` saves an exact pre-attempt journal, performs atomic writes, re-verifies after writing, and restores on any failure.
- `ocx ensure` journals before spawning and stops only its own child when startup/readiness fails.
- Service installation journals before setup and rolls back newly created service state/processes when verification fails.
- Startup stale-injection recovery uses the exact journal while preserving post-injection user edits.
